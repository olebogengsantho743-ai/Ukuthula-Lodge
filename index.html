OUR ENVIRONMENT

Sustainability at Ukuthula

We believe that enjoying nature also means taking care of it. Ukuthula Lodge focuses on practical ways to reduce waste, save resources and support the surrounding community.

🌱

Vegetable Garden

Growing vegetables locally helps reduce our reliance on outside produce.

💧

Filtration System

Our filtration approach helps us make better use of water resources.

🧼

Eco-Based Soaps

We use environmentally conscious soap products wherever possible.

🌿

Harvested Loofah

Natural loofah products provide a more sustainable alternative to synthetic materials.

🤝

Local Businesses

We support local businesses and suppliers within the surrounding community.

🎋

Bamboo

Bamboo can be used as a renewable alternative for selected lodge products and materials.

💡

Motion Sensors

Motion sensors help reduce unnecessary electricity consumption.

🍴

Biodegradable Cutlery

Biodegradable cutlery helps us reduce the use of traditional plastic products.
